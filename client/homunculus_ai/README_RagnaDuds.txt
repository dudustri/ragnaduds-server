Homunculus AI for RagnaDuds: Mir AI Mod v2.2.0 (https://github.com/atomius0/mirai-mod), GPL v2 (license.txt).
Preset so the homunculus hunts by itself: see the top of Config.lua.

Turn it on:  type  /hoai  in the game chat once (again = back to the default AI). The choice is remembered.
Change it:   edit Config.lua in this folder (AI/USER_AI), then  /hoai  twice to reload.
Friends:     move your homunculus (Alt+right click) north or south of a player to add/remove them as a friend
             (it won't steal their monsters). Saved in Friends.txt.
